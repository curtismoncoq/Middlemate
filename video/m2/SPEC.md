# M2 How it works: spec

GOAL:    In 10 s the viewer gets the loop: stake, pay per request, hidden audit, slash.
FORM:    One frame that rebuilds around one object, the real CheapClaude stall (900 px tall, right). Left: one title (136 px Barlow Condensed caps) and a caption of 5 words or fewer per step.
SHOTS:   0-2.5 STAKE: a 5 USDC chip goes into the door; the lights come on amber, placard UNDER REVIEW.
         2.5-5 PAY PER REQUEST: three $0.012 chips land on the price board, 0.4 s apart.
         5-7.5 HIDDEN AUDIT: the real probe c-reverse and qwen's real answer "moonlight"; placard PROBE 1/6..4/6, lamp blinking.
         7.5-10 SLASH: shutter slams (0.45 s power4.out), tape, price struck; STAKE 5 -> 0 in red; held 2.25 s.
MOTION:  titles: per word blur 8 -> 0 in 0.13 s; steps leave with a 0.16 s blur-out; hits are 0 ms sets.
